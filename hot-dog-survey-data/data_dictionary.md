### Data Dictionary

Our data was arranged in the following manner:

| Column                                               | Description                                                                                                 | Potential Reponses                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Year at UVA                                          | The year of the responder                                                                                   | "1st Year", "2nd Year", "3rd Year", "4th Year", "Graduate Student", and "Other/Not Affiliated"                                                                                               |
| What is your Gender Identity?                        | The gender identity of the responder?                                                                       | "Man", "Woman", "Nonbinary", and if the participant was not comfortable answering, they had the option to select "Prefer not to say"                                                             |
| What Schools are you a Part of?                      | The specific UVA school that the student is under?                                                          | Different schools of UVA such as "Arts and Science" or "Engineering"                                                                                                                             |
| Is a Hotdog a Sandwich?                              | The BIG QUESTION, asking whether a hotdog is considered a sandwich                                          | "Yes" means they think a hotdog is a sandwich, "No" means they do not think a hotdog is a sandwich                                                                                               |
| How strongly do you feel about your previous answer? | Asks the responder how confident they are in their answer of whether a hotdog is a sandwich or not          | Integers in the range [1,5], where 1 indicates lowest confidence and 5 indicates highest confidence                                                                                             |
| How frequently do you eat hot dogs?                  | A question to assess the qualifications of the participant. Have they even EATEN a hotdog to know for sure? | Participants could answer "Never", "Rarely (ex. once a year)", "Occassionally (ex. once every few months)", and "Frequently (ex. multiple times a month)", indicating how often they eat hotdogs |
